A taxpayer’s losses from passive activity are limited to passive income from all other sources until disposition of the activity. On disposition, current and prior losses from passive activities can be used but may be subject to the excess business loss limitations.

A rental real estate exception allows taxpayers to deduct up to $25,000 of operating losses from rental real estate annually provided they are actively involved in managing the rental real estate and have adjusted gross income of less than $150,000.
