It’s been a busy year and I’m a busy person, so let me give you the lowdown on my life and you can do your thing. I was unemployed at the beginning of the year and got $2,000 in unemployment compensation. I later got a job as a manager for Roca Cola. I earned $55,000 in base salary this year. My ex-wife, Alice, is still paying me alimony. She’s a lawyer who divorced me in 2015 because I was “unethical” or something like that.
