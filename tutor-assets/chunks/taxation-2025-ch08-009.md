Unlike employees, whose employers withhold tax throughout the year on their behalf, self-employed taxpayers must satisfy their self-employment tax obligations through periodic, usually quarterly, estimated tax payments. Taxpayers who are employed and self-employed (an employee with a business on the side, like Courtney) may have their employers withhold enough taxes to cover their income, self-employment, and additional Medicare tax obligations. Any self-employment or additional Medicare taxes not paid through these mechanisms must be paid with the self-employed taxpayer’s individual tax return.
