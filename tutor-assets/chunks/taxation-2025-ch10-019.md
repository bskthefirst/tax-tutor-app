## CONCLUSION

This chapter describes and discusses how businesses recover the costs of their tangible and intangible assets. Cost recovery is important because it represents a significant tax deduction for many businesses. Businesses must routinely make choices that affect the amount and timing of these deductions. Further understanding cost recovery basics helps businesses determine how to compute and characterize the gain and loss they recognize when they sell or otherwise dispose of business assets. We address the interaction between cost recovery deductions and gain and loss on property dispositions in the next chapter.
