“Large” corporations, defined as corporations with over $1,000,000 of taxable income in any Page 16-29 of the three years prior to the current year,32 may use the prior-year tax liability to determine their first-quarter estimated tax payments only. If they use the prior-year tax liability to determine their first-quarter payment, their second-quarter payment must catch up their estimated payments. That is, the second-quarter payment must be large enough for the sum of the first- and second-quarter payments to equal or exceed 50 percent of the corporation’s projected current-year tax liability.33
