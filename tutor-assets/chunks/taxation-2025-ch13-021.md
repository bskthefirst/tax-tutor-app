Contribution limits generally parallel those for employer-sponsored plans, allowing self-employed individuals to have the same access to qualified plans that employees have.

Self-employed individuals with employees may have to incur the cost of covering their employees when they establish a qualified retirement plan.

- LO 13-6: Compute the saver’s credit. Individuals may receive up to a $1,000 tax credit for contributions they make to IRAs and employer-sponsored qualified plans.

The amount of the credit is a function of the taxpayer’s filing status and AGI.
