## OTHER TAX CHARACTERISTICS

There are many tax factors that differ across entities and can influence the entity selection decision. Exhibit 15-5 provides an overview of these tax characteristics. The exhibit describes the general rules for each tax characteristic as it relates to C corporations, S corporations, entities taxed as partnerships, and sole proprietorships, and it ranks the entities on each characteristic (1 is most tax favorable). Finally, it identifies the chapters where detail on these tax characteristics can be found.
